About Ruth

A lawyer should be able to trace an answer back to the record.

When a client asks whether a notice arrived in time, the answer may turn on a single contract clause and a dated email. Drawing on our work advising and representing clients, we’re building AI tools with legal teams so a lawyer can find those passages, read them in context and decide what they establish.

Jonas Hertner and Joel Niklaus named Ruth after U.S. Supreme Court Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg. The name honors the precision of her arguments and the persistence with which she fought for equal rights for women. They founded Ruth on September 18, 2026, the sixth anniversary of her passing.
