About Ruth

We act as independent counsel and build legal infrastructure within our clients’ organizations.

The matters we handle for a client shape what we build with its legal team. Our lawyers and AI engineers develop tools that connect the team’s advice and decisions to the documents, law and reasoning behind them. When a question comes up again, the team can see why it took a particular course, which assumptions mattered and whether new facts call for different advice.

Four pillars

01

Organizational memory for the legal function

Every position, decision and precedent stays linked to its documents, statutes, case law and factual assumptions, compounding institutional judgment across matters.

02

Task automation and evaluation

We automate recurring legal workflows alongside bespoke evaluation mechanisms that verify accuracy, citations and edge cases against the team’s own standards.

03

Highly customizable user interfaces

We design and build tailored interfaces around how each legal team reviews, decides and collaborates, fitting cleanly into existing tools and matter workflows.

04

Independent legal representation

If instructed, we also represent clients as independent counsel on complex matters, pairing direct legal accountability with the infrastructure built for their teams.

Jonas Hertner and Joel Niklaus named Ruth after U.S. Supreme Court Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg. The name honors her precision in argument, her courage in dissent and her intellectual curiosity. They founded Ruth on September 18, 2026, the sixth anniversary of her passing.
