About Ruth

Ruth combines legal advice and representation with AI engineering. We’re building tools with legal teams to help them find relevant material in their own records, check it against its source and review what the AI produces.

Jonas Hertner and Joel Niklaus named Ruth after U.S. Supreme Court Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg. They founded Ruth on September 18, 2026, the sixth anniversary of her death.
