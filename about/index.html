About Ruth

We act as outside counsel and build legal infrastructure within our clients’ organizations.

The matters we handle for a client shape what we build with its legal team. Our lawyers and AI engineers develop tools that connect the team’s advice and decisions to the documents, law and reasoning behind them. When a question comes up again, the team can see why it took a particular course, which assumptions mattered and whether new facts call for different advice.

Jonas Hertner and Joel Niklaus named Ruth after U.S. Supreme Court Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg. The name honors her precision in argument, her courage in dissent and her intellectual curiosity. They founded Ruth on September 18, 2026, the sixth anniversary of her passing.
