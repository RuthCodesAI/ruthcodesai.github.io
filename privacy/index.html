Privacy notice

This site is built to operate without advertising trackers, behavioral analytics or third-party font requests.

01Cookies and browser storage

We set no cookies and store no browser preferences. Appearance follows your device settings automatically, and fonts and media are served directly from this domain.

02Waitlist signups

If you submit your email address to join the waitlist, we record your address and the time of your consent through our waitlist service hosted on Cloudflare Workers. We use this information solely to send Ruth launch updates and never sell or share it for third-party marketing.

03Hosting and technical logs

Static pages are delivered through GitHub Pages. Standard server connection metadata such as IP address and request timestamp may be processed by the hosting provider solely to deliver the site securely and reliably.

04Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your waitlist entry at any time.
