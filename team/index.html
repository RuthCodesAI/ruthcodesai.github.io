Jonas Hertner

Co-founder, legal practice and product

Jonas represents clients in shareholder and financial disputes, helping them weigh the costs of litigation against a settlement. He spent a decade at Quinn Emanuel, where he helped build the Swiss office and handled cross-border matters. Earlier, he worked at the Court of Appeal of Basel-Stadt. He holds law degrees from the universities of Lucerne and Geneva. In his independent practice, he tests a client’s case against the arguments a regulator or opposing party might make. He wrote The Good Board, a guide for Swiss directors facing decisions about conflicts of interest, dividends and regulatory investigations. He built OpenCaseLaw to make Swiss court decisions freely searchable and link them to the cases and statutes they cite.

Joel Niklaus

Co-founder, legal AI research and engineering

Joel is an expert in legal AI and his datasets and benchmarks such as LEXam, SwiLTra-Bench and LegalBench have shaped the evaluation of LLMs in the legal domain. Previously, he was a Research Scientist at Harvey, specializing in large language model systems for legal applications. Before that, he pretrained LLMs at (Google) X and Thomson Reuters Labs. Joel holds a PhD in NLP from the University of Bern and conducted research at Stanford University, leading projects for the Swiss Federal Supreme Court and AI startups such as Darrow and Libra. His work has been published at top conferences, honored with Outstanding Paper Awards at ACL and CoLM, and covered by Anthropic and the Swiss National Radio & Television.
