Jonas Hertner

Co-founder, legal practice and product

Jonas Hertner is an independent Swiss lawyer based in Zurich and Basel. He represents clients in complex commercial disputes and advises boards and business owners on governance. He worked at the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs International Law Division and the Court of Appeal of Basel-Stadt before spending a decade at Quinn Emanuel, where he helped establish the firm’s Swiss office.

Jonas built OpenCaseLaw, an open dataset that brings together judgments, legislation and regulatory decisions from across Switzerland.

At Ruth, he works with clients to optimize legal tasks and workflows to achieve the highest quality results.

Joel Niklaus

Co-founder, legal AI research and engineering

Joel is an expert in legal AI and his datasets and benchmarks such as LEXam, SwiLTra-Bench and LegalBench have shaped the evaluation of LLMs in the legal domain.

Previously, he was a Research Scientist at Harvey, specializing in large language model systems for legal applications. Before that, he pretrained LLMs at Google X and Thomson Reuters Labs. Joel holds a PhD in NLP from the University of Bern and conducted research at Stanford University, leading projects for the Swiss Federal Supreme Court and AI startups such as Darrow and Libra.

His work has been published at top conferences, honored with Outstanding Paper Awards at ACL and CoLM and covered by Anthropic and the Swiss National Radio and Television.
