Team

Jonas Hertner

Co-founder, legal practice and product

Jonas leads legal practice and product at Ruth. A Swiss lawyer, he spent a decade at Quinn Emanuel, where he helped build the firm’s Swiss office and worked on cross-border disputes. He wrote The Good Board, a guide organized around the decisions Swiss company directors face. He also built and runs OpenCaseLaw, a free research tool that lets readers follow citations from a published Swiss court decision to the judgments and statutory provisions it cites.

Joel Niklaus

Co-founder, legal AI research and engineering

Joel leads legal AI research and engineering at Ruth. He earned his PhD in natural language processing at the University of Bern. His work took him from training language models at Alphabet’s X lab and Thomson Reuters Labs to developing systems for legal work at Harvey. At Hugging Face he works on synthetic training data and the software that guides AI agents. He co-created MultiLegalPile; the paper won an ACL 2024 Outstanding Paper Award.
