Team

Jonas Hertner

Co-founder, legal practice and product

Jonas represents clients in shareholder and financial disputes, helping them weigh the costs of litigation against a settlement. He spent a decade at Quinn Emanuel, where he helped build the Swiss office and handled cross-border matters. In his independent practice, he tests clients’ positions against the arguments a regulator or an opposing party might make. He wrote The Good Board, a guide to decisions Swiss boards face, and built OpenCaseLaw, where lawyers can read Swiss court decisions alongside the cases and statutory provisions they cite.

Joel Niklaus

Co-founder, legal AI research and engineering

Joel helped create two open tests: LEXam to compare models on law school exams and SwiLTra-Bench to check their translations of Swiss laws and court headnotes. He contributed to LegalBench, where lawyers can compare models on reasoning tasks designed by their peers, and to LEXTREME, which compares performance across 24 languages. Joel and his co-authors received Outstanding Paper Awards for MultiLegalPile, a collection of legal texts for training models (ACL 2024), and for their FinePhrase study on synthetic training data (COLM 2026).
